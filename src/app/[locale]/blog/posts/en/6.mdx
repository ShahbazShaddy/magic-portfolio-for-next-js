## Overview

The 2026 NCTS Summer School on Theoretical Approaches to Quantum Geometry, Nonlinear Responses, and Topological Phenomena in Quantum Systems provided an advanced introduction to modern theoretical concepts in quantum and condensed matter physics. The summer school focused on the connections between quantum geometry, Berry curvature, topology, symmetry, nonlinear responses, and observable physical properties in quantum systems. It covered topics related to topological phases of matter, nonlinear transport and optical phenomena, correlated quantum states, moiré and low-dimensional materials, and photonic and synthetic quantum systems. Through lectures by researchers from Taiwan and abroad, participants gained insights into how quantum-mechanical and geometric properties of materials can influence their electronic and optical responses, while also developing a broader understanding of contemporary research in quantum materials and theoretical condensed matter physics.
